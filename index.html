How it works

The strategy is simple: stay out while the game is uncertain, and step in once it's almost decided.

STEP 1

Watch everything live

The bot tracks live scores from several data sources and every matching market, around the clock.

STEP 2

Wait for the finish

It only acts in the last minutes of a match, when the leader is a set up and clearly ahead in the deciding set.

STEP 3

Trade with strict limits

It uses small, fixed bet sizes, caps how much is at risk, and pauses automatically after a loss. Every trade is logged with the live score.

The bot's rules

  • Price of 94¢ or higher, so the market has to agree it's nearly over
  • Two live sources must agree on the score before any trade
  • No tiebreaks, injuries or suspensions. When in doubt, it skips
  • Fixed size per bet, with a daily cap
  • Automatic pause after a single losing bet

What's published here

  • Every settled bet, wins and losses, per strategy
  • Win rate and ROI after fees, updated automatically
  • Live top-trader dashboard from Polymarket's public data
  • Read-only. No sign-ups, no payments, no picks for sale
